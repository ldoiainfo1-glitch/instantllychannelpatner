How to Become an IPNL-Channel Partner

Complete guide on using the IPNL Channel Partner Website

1
Find a Position

Search by zone, state, district, tehsil, pincode, or village to find an open Channel Partner position available in your area..

2
Apply Now

Click "Apply Now" on your chosen position and fill in your details — name, phone number, pincode, business/company name, photo, and referrer (optional).

3
Get Approved

Your application is reviewed by the admin team. Once approved, your unique Channel Partner ID is generated instantly.

4
Refer & Grow

Onboard more partners under your ID. Once you cross 6 members at your level (e.g. Village), you get promoted to the next level up (Pincode → Tehsil → District → Division → State → Zone).

How to Use IPNL

Complete guide on using the India Property Network

Discover

Browse off-market opportunities filtered by city, asset class, ticket size and mandate type — all from verified principals.

Verify

Every firm is KYC'd and tier-rated. See verification status, firm credentials and track record before reaching out.

Connect

Request an introduction or message the principal directly. Take the conversation forward off-platform — no brokerage held by India Property Network Ltd.

How to Use Co-Brokings

Complete guide on using the Co-Brokings network

Post

Pick a side — Buy, Sell, Rent or Lease — and type your requirement in plain text. No photos, no forms.

Broadcast

Your post instantly reaches every broker in the city and area circles you've joined. Auto-join your city, opt into areas you work.

Co-broke

Matching broker taps Chat. You message inside the app. Phone unmasks the moment both of you accept share.
